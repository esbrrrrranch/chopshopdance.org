Festival Performances

The Festival Performances are held at the Meydenbauer Center Theatre, located in downtown Bellevue.

Meydenbauer Center Theatre

11100 NE 6th Street
Bellevue, WA 98004
View Map

By Car:

The parking garage at The Bravern offers up to 6 hours of parking free of charge with merchant validation. The Bravern is located adjacent to Meydenbauer Center at 11111 NE 8th Street. The parking garage can be accessed either from 110th Avenue NE or NE 8th Street. For more information on parking at The Bravern, please visit thebravern.com/the-bravern-parking.

Alternatively, Meydenbauer Center offers pay parking in its garage for visitors. The garage entrance is accessible from NE 6th Street. The garage provides a total of nine ADA accessible parking stalls, several on each floor of the garage in close proximity to building elevators. For more information on traveling to and from Meydenbauer Center Theatre, please visit meydenbauer.com/parking-directions.

By Public Transportation:

Several bus routes serve the Bellevue Transit Center, located a short walk from Meydenbauer Center Theatre. You may use King County Metro's online Trip Planner tool or call 206.553.3000 to plan your trip.

CHOP SHOP Master Class Series

The CHOP SHOP Master Class Series is held at two locations: Velocity Dance Center (February 13) and on stage at Meydenbauer Center Theatre (February 15 & 16). Information on each venue is included below. Please visit the Master Class Series page for class schedules, registration, and scholarship information.

February 13 - Velocity Dance Center

1621 12th Ave #100
Seattle, WA 98122
View Map
Velocity Dance Center website

By car:

Velocity Dance Center is located on 12th Ave. between E. Olive St. and E. Pine St. Metered street parking is available and there are several paid parking lots within walking distance of the studios. Check estimated parking rates and find a lot at Parkopedia.

By Public Transportation:

Velocity Dance Center is within walking distance of several bus stops and the Capitol Hill Light Rail Station. Learn more about public transit options by using King County Metro's online Trip Planner tool or by calling 206.553.3000.

Experience Dance Program

Experience Dance Program classes are held at three Bellevue locations: Bellevue College, Crossroads Community Center, and Bellevue Arts Museum. Information on each venue is included below. Please visit the Experience Dance Program page for class schedules and registration information.

Crossroads Community Center

16000 NE 10th Street
Bellevue, WA 98008
View Map

By Car:

Crossroads Community Center offers free parking in the lot in front of their building, including ADA accessible parking spaces.

By Public Transportation:

Crossroads Community Center is within walking distance of several bus stops. Learn more about public transit options by using King County Metro's online Trip Planner tool or by calling 206.553.3000.

Bellevue College

3000 Landerholm Circle Southeast
Building E - Stopgap Theater Bellevue, WA 98007
View Map
View Bellevue College Campus Map

By Car:

We recommend that you park in Lot 12 for easy access to the venue (lot location shown on the Bellevue College Campus Map). Lot 12 includes ADA accessible parking spaces.

By Public Transportation:

Bellevue College is served by several bus routes and is located within walking distance of the Eastgate Park & Ride. You can view a map of bus stop locations here: www.bellevuecollege.edu/location/maps/alternative. Learn more about public transit options by using King County Metro's online Trip Planner tool or by calling 206.553.3000.

Bellevue Arts Museum

510 Bellevue Way NE
Bellevue, WA 98004
View Map

By Car:

Bellevue Arts Museum offers free parking in its garage for visitors. The garage closes 30 minutes after the museum. The garage entrance is accessible when traveling south on Bellevue Way.

By Public Transportation:

Several bus routes serve the Bellevue Transit Center, located a short walk from Bellevue Arts Museum. You may use King County Metro's online Trip Planner tool or call 206.553.3000 to plan your trip.

For more information on traveling to and from Bellevue Arts Museum, please visit bellevuearts.org/visit.
