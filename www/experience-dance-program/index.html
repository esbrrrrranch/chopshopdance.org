Experience Dance Program

Free. Every age, every ability. No experience necessary.

The Experience Dance Program is Inlet Arts' community offering, and it runs in the weeks preceding the CHOP SHOP festival weekend:

  • Reading Dance — an hour-long lecture-demonstration on the creative process behind contemporary dance

  • Introduction to Contemporary Dance — free movement classes open to all

  • Open Studio Sessions — behind-the-scenes access to professional rehearsals

There is no audition, no dress code, and no prerequisite. Since 2008, more than 1,000 people have taken part.

The Experience Dance Program runs year-round, across all of Inlet Arts' work — in community centers, libraries, and schools across the Eastside. See the full program at Inlet Arts →
