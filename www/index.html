CHOP SHOP returns April 23-25, 2027

After fourteen festivals and a pause, the Eastside's contemporary dance festival comes back for its fifteenth year. Meydenbauer Center Theatre, Bellevue, WA.

CHOP SHOP: Bodies of Work is a contemporary dance festival founded in 2008 by StoneDance Productions and now produced by Inlet Arts . We bring choreographers from the Eastside, across the country, and around the world to Bellevue — with master classes, post-show conversations, and free community classes open to every age and ability.
