The First Fourteen Years

CHOP SHOP: Bodies of Work presented fourteen festivals between 2008 and 2021, bringing contemporary dance from the Eastside, Seattle, and around the world to audiences in Bellevue. The fourteenth—reimagined as an online festival of dance film—ran through the winter of 2021. During these years, CHOP SHOP was produced by StoneDance Productions , Eva Stone’s company. The festival paused after that season.

CHOP SHOP returns April 23–25, 2027, led by Emily Schoen Branch and Eva Stone, as a program of Inlet Arts .
